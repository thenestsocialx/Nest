Why it matters

A Quiet Pandemic.

It doesn't make the news like a virus. There's no test and no vaccine. But it spreads through cities, hostels, offices and family WhatsApp groups, and it shows up in our bodies.

According to the WHO, loneliness and social isolation raise the risk of:

StrokeHeart diseaseDiabetesCognitive declineEarly death

2023

The US Surgeon General declares loneliness an epidemic.

May 2025

The World Health Assembly adopts its first‑ever resolution on social connection.

June 2025

The WHO calls social connection a global public health priority.

Today

The Nest Social is building the answer here in India. Starting with people, not robots.

The good news

Connection Is One of the Most Powerful Things We Have.

The same WHO report found that strong social connection can lower the risk of serious health problems, support mental health and help prevent early death. Companionship isn't a luxury. It's how humans are built.

And it can't be bought, shipped or switched on. It's built, one relationship at a time, starting with the one we have with ourselves.

1

Understand Yourself.

What you want, how you show up, and the patterns you keep repeating. The clearer you are, the better you connect.

2

Friends, family, partners. Learn to say what you mean, listen better and have the conversations that matter.

3

Choose Who You Let In.

When you know yourself, you choose better. Relationships built that way tend to last.
